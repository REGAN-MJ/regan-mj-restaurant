REGAN MJ RESTAURANT

Our Story

Regan MJ Restaurant began as a small neighbourhood café with a big idea: to bring delicious, thoughtfully-prepared meals to our community while celebrating local ingredients and warm hospitality. Over the years we have grown into a multi-location restaurant group across Kampala and Entebbe, known for our fresh flavours, welcoming atmosphere, and commitment to quality.

Our kitchen blends contemporary techniques with traditional recipes to create dishes that are familiar yet exciting. We work with local suppliers to source seasonal produce and ensure every plate reflects care and craftsmanship.

Mission & Values

  • Quality: Serve fresh, consistently excellent food.
  • Community: Support local producers and create great local jobs.
  • Sustainability: Reduce waste and choose eco-friendly suppliers where possible.
  • Service: Make every guest feel welcome and celebrated.

Community & Sustainability

We support local farms through a direct-supply program and minimise waste through portion control and composting initiatives. We organize community events and pro-bono catering for local charities throughout the year.
